rnaseq-flow · UCL ARC

You choose one aligner per run with one flag, and the downstream stages adapt to it. STAR is the default, and its sorted BAMs unlock the most: featureCounts, RSeQC, coverage tracks, rMATS, exon-level diffSplice and fusion calling. HISAT2 is the lower-memory genome route with the same downstream stack minus STAR-Fusion. Worth saying: on nf-core/rnaseq, HISAT2 gives you a BAM and no expression estimates; here featureCounts runs after either genome aligner, so HISAT2 is a lighter route to the same analysis, not a dead end. Salmon and Kallisto are the fast transcript-level routes, summarised to genes by tximport, and they unlock transcript usage; isoform switching is Salmon only. Since 1.8.0 the transcriptome they index is cut from the genome and GTF with gffread, so index and annotation describe the same transcripts by construction.
