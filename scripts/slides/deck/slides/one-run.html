rnaseq-flow · UCL ARC

This is the honest comparison with nf-core, which most people here will know. nf-core gives you four excellent specialist pipelines: rnaseq for QC and counts, differentialabundance for statistics and pathways, rnasplice and rnafusion. That means four runs, separate samplesheets, a contrasts file, and references to keep consistent between them. rnaseq-flow answers all of it in one run, from one samplesheet, with every analysis oriented against the same baseline. To be fair in the other direction: nf-core/rnaseq is the better front half. It has UMI handling, rRNA and contaminant removal, more QC tools and far more testing. Some of that is on my roadmap, and I'll come back to it. This comparison is from nf-core's own documentation; I haven't run it side by side.
