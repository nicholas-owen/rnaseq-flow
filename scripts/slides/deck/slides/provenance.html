rnaseq-flow · UCL ARC

Third, provenance, and this is the part I care most about as a data steward. It is on by default, because provenance that has to be remembered isn't provenance. The run manifest records how the run was configured: version and commit, the command line, the aligner, the baseline, the thresholds, the design, the samples per condition, and the reference set with its Ensembl release. The Workflow Run RO-Crate says the same in standard JSON-LD that WorkflowHub and other tools can read: one action per task with its inputs and outputs, a checksum for every published file, and the pipeline itself as it ran. And it records people. Each contributor is a Person with an ORCID behind CRediT roles as resolvable URIs, it says whether a role was stated or inferred from who launched the run, and the project carries a RAiD, funder, grant and rights holder. Identifiers are checked at launch. nf-core ships a crate describing its code; this one describes your run.
